# Bolo YouTube demo script (about 90 seconds)

**0:00–0:15 — The problem**  
“Reporting a broken streetlight or a water leak can mean opening a website, finding the right form and typing a lot of details. Bolo explores a simpler approach: speak naturally.”

**0:15–0:30 — Start the experience**  
“Here is Bolo. I tap the microphone and describe the issue: ‘There is a broken streetlight near City Park, Sector 12. It is dangerous and needs attention urgently.’”

**0:30–0:50 — Show the voice-to-form flow**  
“Bolo converts my speech into a structured report. It identifies the issue category, location phrase and urgency. I can edit any field, so I stay in control.”

**0:50–1:10 — Confirm**  
“I review the details, confirm the demo report, and Bolo creates a reference receipt. The app can also read the confirmation aloud.”

**1:10–1:25 — Be transparent**  
“This is a working hackathon prototype. It does not yet submit complaints to a municipal authority. A real launch would need an authorised civic integration, privacy and security review, and testing with residents.”

**1:25–1:35 — Close**  
“Bolo: your voice, your choice, your report. Speak up. Get things moving.”

## Recording checklist
- Record in Chrome or Edge and allow microphone permission.
- Use a quiet room and test the example phrase before recording.
- Keep the browser zoom at 90–100% so the full experience is visible.
- Show the editable fields and the demo-only receipt disclaimer.
- Keep the final video under 3 minutes and upload it as public or unlisted.
